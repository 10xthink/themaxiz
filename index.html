Questions, answered

Do I need a website?

No. These services are designed for businesses that promote and manage enquiries through WhatsApp and social media.

Can I start with just one flyer?

Yes. You can enquire about one introductory flyer before choosing a larger package.

Will you access my WhatsApp account?

We can guide you through setup on your own device. You should never share passwords, verification codes or account access credentials.

Can I use my own business photos and logo?

Yes. Share the photos and branding you own or have permission to use when discussing your project.

Is payment taken on this site?

No. This site prepares an enquiry only. Final pricing and payment arrangements are confirmed separately.
